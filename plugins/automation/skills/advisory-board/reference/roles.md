# Role library

Pick three to five roles per board. Always include the reader and the
fact-checker; fill the rest from the output type below. These are starting
points, not a closed list: if the user's field isn't here, write roles for
it with the rule at the bottom of this file. Rewrite a role's
mandate to fit the situation: "the skeptical buyer" for a CFO reading a
proposal is not the same person as for a developer reading docs.

## Roles every board gets

| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The reader** | Reads it the way the real reader will: busy, distracted, comparing it with alternatives. Does it make me do the thing it wants me to do? Where did I stop reading? |
| **The fact-checker** | Every number, name, date, quote and promise has a source the reader could check. Flags anything unsourced, rounded beyond recognition, or out of date. |

## By output type

### Web content, AEO and SEO pages
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The answer engine** | Could an AI assistant lift a clean, correct answer from this page for the question it targets? Is the answer near the top, stated plainly, with the entity named? |
| **The searcher** | Typed the question into Google or ChatGPT five seconds ago. Does the first screen answer it, or make them scroll past an intro? |
| **The editor** | Cuts filler, repetition, throat-clearing and AI tells ("in today's fast-paced world", "delve", lists of three for rhythm). Checks the house style. |
| **The competitor** | Has the best page on this topic open next to it. What do they have that this page doesn't? What would make a reader switch tabs? |

### Technical audits and data analyses
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The reproducer** | Re-runs the query or filter behind every finding. A count it can't reproduce is a blocker. |
| **The client's developer** | Can the client's CMS and stack actually express this fix, and who has to do it? |
| **The prioritiser** | Is the order by impact on traffic and revenue, or by how easy it was to find? |

### Client reports and recommendations
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The client stakeholder who has to act on it** | What do I do on Monday? Is this specific to us, or would every client get the same sentence? |
| **The data checker** | Every number matches the source export, and month-over-month changes are computed, not estimated. |
| **The account manager** | Can I defend every line in the call? Does it contradict what we told this client before? |

A shared charter for client reports keeps quality the same across the team,
whoever writes the report.

### Briefs and plans for other people to execute
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The person executing it** | Could I start tomorrow without asking a single question? What's ambiguous, missing, or contradictory? |
| **The owner of the outcome** | Does this serve the goal, or just the task? What would make me say "that's not what I meant"? |

### Emails, proposals, client communication
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The skeptical buyer** | What would make me not reply? Which claim do I not believe? What did I need to hear that isn't here? |
| **The account lead** | Is this consistent with what we've told this client before? Any promise we can't keep, any tone that doesn't fit the relationship? |
| **The lawyer** | Commitments, guarantees, prices, deadlines, data use. Anything here we'd regret in writing? |

### Code and technical changes
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The maintainer** | Will someone understand and change this in six months? Reuse over new code, no special cases that a general mechanism would cover. |
| **The attacker** | Inputs, secrets, permissions, injection, data leaving the system. |
| **The operator** | What happens when it fails at 3 a.m.? Logs, retries, rollbacks, cost. |

### Automation and process designs
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The person whose job this was** | What does the design not know that I know? Which edge case happens every week? |
| **The operator** | Where does it break silently? Who notices, and how? What happens when an API key expires? |
| **The security officer** | Which credentials does it hold, how are they scoped and revoked, what can it delete or send? |

### People and HR (job ads, feedback, policies)
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The candidate or employee** | Reads it as the person it's about. Would I apply, trust it, feel treated fairly? |
| **The employment lawyer** | Discrimination, promises, wording that creates obligations. |
| **The manager who has to live with it** | Can I actually run my team this way? |

### Finance and reporting (board updates, forecasts, budgets)
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The CFO** | Do the numbers tie out? Are assumptions stated and sensible? |
| **The board member** | What's the decision you want from me, and is the bad news on page one? |
| **The auditor** | Could every figure be traced back to its source? |

### Sales (proposals, follow-ups, outreach)
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The buyer's boss** | The buyer forwards this. Does it make the case without them in the room? |
| **The competitor's sales rep** | Where would I attack this offer? |
| **Delivery** | Can we actually deliver what's promised, on that timeline, at that price? |

### Operations and support (processes, help articles, replies)
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The frustrated customer** | Did this solve my problem in the first two sentences? |
| **The new hire** | Could I follow this on my first day without asking anyone? |

### Product and research (specs, studies, analyses)
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The user** | Is this the problem I have, in my words? |
| **The methodologist** | Does the evidence support the conclusion? Sample, bias, alternative explanations. |
| **The engineer** | What's unclear or impossible to build as written? |

### Teaching and training
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The learner** | Where do I get lost? What did you assume I already know? |
| **The expert** | What's oversimplified to the point of being wrong? |

### Decks and talks
| Role | Mandate |
|---|---|
| **The person in row five** | Can I read it from here? Do I get the point of each slide from its headline alone? |
| **The bored expert** | I've heard this talk before. Where's the part I haven't? |

## Writing your own role

A good role has a stake and a question. "Marketing expert" has neither.
"The head of sales who has to use this page in a call next week, and will
be embarrassed if a prospect finds a mistake" has both.
